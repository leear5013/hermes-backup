# Europe Scholarship Catalog (Egypt-based students) — verified pass 2026-08-11

Target profile: Egyptian, Data Engineering bachelor, graduation June 2026 → targeting **2027 entry** (applications open fall 2026). Deadlines shift yearly — re-verify on official sources before quoting.

## Fully-funded programs (ranked by odds × fit for Egyptians)

### 1. Stipendium Hungaricum 🇭🇺 (best realistic fully-funded)
- Full tuition + monthly stipend (~HUF 43,700/mo ≈ €110) + dorm/accommodation contribution + health insurance.
- Egypt has a **bilateral agreement** → dedicated Egyptian quotas, far better odds than Erasmus.
- English-taught AI / data / computer science programs at University of Debrecen, ELTE, BME, Szeged, etc.
- Application window for 2027/28: **~Oct 2026 – Jan 15, 2027** (via apply.stipendiumhungaricum.hu, nominated through the Egyptian embassy/MOHE).
- Official: stipendiumhungaricum.hu

### 2. Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (EMJM) 🇪🇺 (stretch — top quality, worst odds)
- Up to fully funded: tuition + travel + installation costs + ~€1,400/month allowance. Study in 2–3 countries.
- AI-relevant programs exist across the catalogue (e.g. MSc AI consortia); Italy/France/Spain all appear as consortium partners.
- Acceptance ~1–5%. **No central portal** — apply per-program.
- Application window for 2027 entry: **Oct 2026 – Jan 2027** per program.
- Official: erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu → Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters; catalogue at erasmuscatalogue.com (37 new EMJM selected July 2026 for 2027).

### 3. DAAD (Germany) 🇩🇪
- **EPOS** (development-related master's, incl. some tech/data programs): fully funded. Application ~Aug–Sep 2026 for 2027.
- Alternative framing: public German master's are **tuition-free** (~€0), need blocked account €11,904/yr + 140 legal work days/yr — self-funded but cheap. 18-month post-study job-seeker visa.
- Heilbronn AI Fellowship (newer, research-oriented, postdoc-heavy).
- Official: daad.de

### 4. Fulbright Egypt 🇺🇸 (not Europe, but the tweet-thread's top recommendation)
- Fully funded US master's; Egypt-dedicated slots; separate from the general US program.
- Application cycle for Egypt typically ~May–June for the following year.
- Official: egypt.usembassy.gov / fulbright-egypt.org

### 5. Invest Your Talent in Italy (IYT) 🇮🇹
- MAECI/ICE/Uni-Italia program: scholarships for English-taught engineering incl. AI/computer science at Politecnico di Milano, Torino, etc.
- 2026/27 call: applications opened ~April 2026, deadline **11 May 2026**. Expect similar ~spring window for 2027/28.
- Competition notably lower than Erasmus Mundus.
- Official: investyourtalentapplication.esteri.it

### 6. Eiffel Excellence Scholarship 🇫🇷
- €1,031/month + international travel + local transport + health cover; masters up to 12–24 months.
- **Must be nominated by a French university** — candidate cannot self-apply; secure a university admission/nomination first (university noms ~Nov–Dec 2026 for 2027 intake; e.g. ENS Lyon deadline Nov 22 for 2026/27).
- Official: campusfrance.org/fr/france-excellence-eiffel-scholarship-program

### 7. VLIR-UOS ICP Connect (Belgium 🇧🇪)
- Fully funded 1-year advanced/specialization masters for developing-country students (Egypt eligible) at Flemish universities (KU Leuven, UGent, etc.). Check the ICP program list for AI/data fit — many programs are development/social-science oriented.
- Official: vliruos.be/get-funded/study-scholarships

### 8. Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals 🇸🇪
- Full-ride (tuition + living stipend + travel); must first apply to an eligible master's via universityadmissions.se (deadline ~mid-Jan), then SI portal window ~Feb 9–25.
- 12-month post-study residence permit for job search.
- Official: si.se

## Netherlands 🇳🇱 (user specifically asked — "something like netherland")

Reality check: non-EEA tuition €15–20k/yr + living €1,300–1,500/mo. Scholarships cover PART of it; NL is a **job-outcome play** (ASML, Booking.com, NXP, Philips — strong data/AI hiring; Orientation Year visa = 1 yr job hunt → highly-skilled migrant route).

- **NL Scholarship** (formerly Holland Scholarship): €5,000 one-time, non-EEA, university-set deadlines between **Feb 1 – May 1** per university (e.g. Twente May 1 Round 2). Applications open ~Nov 1. Official: studyinnl.org
- **University of Twente Scholarship**: €3,000–€22,000/yr (distinct from NL Scholarship).
- **TU Delft Excellence/JUST/van Effen**: full or partial tuition for outstanding students.
- **Amsterdam Merit Scholarship** (UvA), **Radboud/Leiden/Groningen** excellence schemes: €5–15k partial.
