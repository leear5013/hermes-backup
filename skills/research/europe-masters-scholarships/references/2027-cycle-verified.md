## Program route facts (per scholarship)
- **NL Scholarship (Holland Scholarship)**: €5,000, one year, non-EEA only. No national deadline —
  each university sets its own, typically **1 Feb – 1 May 2026** for 2026/27 (2027/28 cycle:
  opened 1 Nov 2025 per studyinnl.org — verify per-uni for 2027/28). studyinnl.org/finances/nl-scholarship
- **Swedish Institute SISGP**: apply to eligible master's on universityadmissions.se first
  (deadline ~15 Jan), then SI portal **9–25 Feb**. Full-ride.
- **Invest Your Talent in Italy**: MAECI + ICE + Uni-Italia; 2026/27 call deadline was 11 May 2026
  (en.unimib.it news). English-taught engineering programs.
- **Eiffel (France)**: university must nominate you; uni-side deadlines ~Nov/Dec 2025 for 2026/27
  (ENS Lyon: 22 Nov 2025; UBE: 5 Dec 2025). ~€1,031/mo masters.
- **VLIR-UOS ICP Connect (Belgium)**: developing-country quota incl. Egypt; one-year advanced
  masters; vliruos.be/get-funded/study-scholarships.
- **DAAD**: EPOS for development-related programs (~Aug–Sep); German public universities otherwise
  tuition-free; blocked account ~€11,900/yr.
- **Stipendium Hungaricum**: Egypt bilateral partner; study finder:
  stipendiumhungaricum.hu/study-finder/ (filter "Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship Programme: available").
  Full tuition + monthly stipend + housing contribution. 2027/28 call typically opens Oct,
  deadline ~15 Jan.
- **Fulbright Egypt**: standard rec for never-traveled Egyptian students; fully funded US master's;
  Egypt program application ~May–June.
