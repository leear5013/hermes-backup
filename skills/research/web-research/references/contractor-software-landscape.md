# Contractor Software Landscape (2026-08)

## Construction PM Software

| Product | Pricing | Target | Key Weakness |
|---------|---------|--------|--------------|
| BuilderTrend | Custom (sales call required) | Mid-large builders (5+ projects/yr) | "Clickertrend" estimating, rigid contracts |
| Procore | Custom ACV-based, unlimited users | Large commercial/residential | Prices out small contractors |
| Jobber | $49-$499/mo, transparent | Small-mid field service | Less robust for complex builds |
| CoConstruct | Merged into BuilderTrend | Custom home builders | No longer independent |

### Construction Pain Points
- Estimating tools are click-heavy and inflexible (BuilderTrend)
- Annual contracts with auto-renewal, hard to exit (BuilderTrend)
- Gap between spreadsheet and enterprise PM for 2-5 project/year builders

## Field Service Software (Plumbing/Electrical/HVAC)

| Product | Pricing | Target | Key Weakness |
|---------|---------|--------|--------------|
| ServiceTitan | $200-400+/tech/month (custom) | Mid-large (10+ techs) | Expensive, app crashes, rigid contracts |
| Housecall Pro | $59-$169/mo (actual $80+) | Small-mid (1-10 techs) | Aggressive upselling, unauthorized changes |
| Jobber | $49-$499/mo | Small (1-20 employees) | Less feature-rich for large ops |
| Workiz | Free-$320/mo | Small-mid | Smaller company, fewer integrations |
| FieldEdge | Custom (demo required) | Mid-sized | Custom pricing only |

### Field Service Pain Points
- Nickel-and-diming add-ons (HCP, ServiceTitan)
- Unauthorized account changes — AI messages sent without consent (HCP)
- Payment processing delays and app crashes (HCP, ServiceTitan)

## Micro-SaaS Opportunities
1. Transparent $29-99/mo pricing, no lock-in contracts
2. Simple construction estimating for small builders
3. Reliable payments without unauthorized feature changes
4. No aggressive upselling, stable UI
