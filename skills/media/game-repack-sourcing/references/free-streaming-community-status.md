## Community warnings
- Some listed sites "pause to load literally every 30 seconds" even on good
  connections — flag quality risk when recommending.
- One site was delisted for "very inappropriate stuff in their discord +
  forcing discord logins" — discord-login walls are a hygiene red flag.
- Lists churn fast; ALWAYS re-check a site is alive before handing it over
  (curl the homepage / the specific show page).

## Finding a specific show (workflow that worked)
- web_search: `<show> مسلسل مترجم فشار سيما` (Arabic-censored sites) AND
  `<show> watch online free` (international) — run BOTH; they return
  different site sets.
- r/Piracy rss-top + ArcticShift comment search on the monthly FMHY thread
  gives the current alive/dead status.
- For shows on Netflix: international pirate mirrors carry the UNCENSORED
  upload. Arabic censored sites carry the cut version but often lack the
  full episode or recent shows. There is NO free site that both has the full
  episode AND strips +18 — tell the user plainly: pick full (skip manually)
  or cut (Arabic sites), not both.

## Egyptian/Arabic censored streaming (cut of +18 by law)
- Fushaar (fushaar.link / s.fushar.video), CimaLeek (cimaleek.pw),
  Cima4U, QFilm (a.qfilm.tv), CimaLight, TopCinema, CimaNow, Leftaaa,
  aflaam.com. Free, no login. Versions are censored by the Egyptian
  censorship board — no nude scenes, but sometimes plot-relevant cuts.
- Beware: censored sites often host only partial episodes or miss recent
  titles — verify the exact episode exists and is full-length.
